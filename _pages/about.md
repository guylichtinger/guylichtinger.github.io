I am a second-year PhD student in economics at Harvard University. My research lies at the intersection of labor economics, public economics, and the economics of AI. Currently, I am most interested in the effects of AI on labor market outcomes and inequality. 

Prior to Harvard, I earned an MA in Economics and a BA in Philosophy, Political Science, and Economics (PPE) from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.

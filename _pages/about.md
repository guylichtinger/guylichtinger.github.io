I am a PhD student in Economics at Harvard University. My research focuses on labor economics and public economics, with a particular interest in inequality, gender, and technological change.

My current work studies how generative AI is reshaping the structure of the labor market — in particular, how it affects the demand for junior versus senior workers and what this implies for early-career trajectories. In related work, I study the transmission of family culture across generations and its consequences for gender gaps in the labor market.

Before starting at Harvard, I completed my graduate studies in economics at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem as part of the Bogen International Graduate Program.
